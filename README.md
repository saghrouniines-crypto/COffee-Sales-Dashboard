# Coffee Sales Dashboard 

##  Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Excel dashboard** analyzing coffee sales data from **2019–2022**.  
It highlights performance by coffee type, country, and customer loyalty, offering insights into sales trends and top customers.

## 🔎 Key Insights
- **United States** leads with total sales of **$9,653**, accounting for the majority of revenue.
- **Excella** coffee type generated the highest sales (**$3,427**), followed by **Liberica**.
- Seasonal spikes observed in **February** and **June** across multiple years.
- Top customers contributed significantly, with **Terri Farra** leading at **$168** in sales.
- Loyalty program customers show consistent repeat purchases.

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques
- **Excel Pivot Tables** for aggregating sales by year, month, and coffee type.
- **Charts & Visualizations** (line graphs, bar charts) for trend analysis.
- **Data Cleaning** to ensure consistency in customer and product records.
- **Storytelling with Data** to highlight actionable insights.

##  How to Use
1. Download `coffeeOrdersData.xlsx`.
2. Open in **Microsoft Excel** (2016 or later recommended).
3. Explore the interactive dashboard:
   - Filter by year or coffee type.
   - Review country‑level sales.
   - Identify top customers and loyalty patterns.
